Setup — bearings along the bar

Bearing 1 = 0", nearest the drive. Add the rest as distance from it.
Bores in this setup — same reference as the bearings.
How this is calculated
The cutter travels through each bore, crossing whichever bearings lie along the way. Every sub-span the cutter passes through is checked — including local worst points inside a span, not just the ends of the bore — plus cantilever past the last bearing if a bore extends beyond it. Multi-bearing layouts (e.g. a BB5000 wide span with a mid-bearing) are handled directly rather than approximated as a near/far pair.

Open End / Blind Bore — bearing stacking

Last bore has support on one side only. The bar cantilevers past the final bearing to reach the cut, so the stack behind it is all the rigidity you get.
What this recommendation uses

Line boring cutting insert — bore & rotation

Bore diameter and material come from the shared Job Context above. This is the boring machine's cutting insert SFM — separate from the bore welder's rotation calculator, which controls weld deposition rate for a completely different reason.
Formula: SFM = (Bore diameter × RPM) ÷ 3.82, and RPM = (SFM × 3.82) ÷ Bore diameter — 3.82 is the rounded constant for 12 ÷ π.
